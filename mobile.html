Top slider: RA-to-Pure blend (0% = single-year Risk-Adj, 100% = single-year Pure Ceiling on the same pace basis the Pure trajectory uses). Bottom slider: Career years (1-10). At 1, it shows the single-season value. At 10, it shows expected RA summed across the next 10 seasons (career value); in Single season mode it shows the engine's own year-10 trajectory, with no floor under a year a player has aged out of. Combine the two for "5-year career value at 30% Pure blend" etc. Verdict: the percentage is of the LARGER side — under 5% reads as fair, 5 to 15% a slight edge, above 15% a clear win. A gap worth less than five cents of value is called even rather than graded.

Each colored segment is one player's injury discount on his asset value: his healthy RA (Pure × Pace × Hit%) minus his risk-adjusted RA — what the Durability v2.2 module takes off (rate after a return, the UCL workload factor, survival, recurrence load, and a 0.90-per-season deferral discount for production pushed back by a long absence; the coming season's availability itself is priced on the season line, not the headline). Color = share of value removed: Yellow under 5% (low), Orange 5–15% (moderate), Red 15–35% (high), Purple 35%+ (extreme). Players the module leaves untouched (×1.00) are left off. Bar height = total RA a side is giving up to injury.
